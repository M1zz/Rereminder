Rereminder 2.2.4

Your timer can now end with an alarm. It rings at alarm volume even in silent mode or a Focus, and keeps going until you stop it. Turn it on under Notifications in Settings.

The dial now gives haptic feedback. You can feel the time change without looking at the screen.

Fixed a bug where choosing vibration meant no alert at all. Both iPhone and Apple Watch now respond as they should.

Added Things you may have missed at the top of Notifications in Settings. Read what a feature does and turn it on right there.

Fixed a few untranslated phrases and simplified the device connection indicators.
