Rereminder 2.2.2

The pre-alert limit is gone. Set as many as you want, free.

Pro is one thing now. The app remembers your setups. That covers saved templates, the dial you used last, session mode, overtime and your history. On the free plan the dial returns to its default when you fully quit and relaunch. Templates you already saved stay where they are, locked.

You can add Rereminder to the Apple Watch Smart Stack. See the time left and the next alert without opening the app. It works as a watch face complication too.

The watch timer screen was redrawn. The ring follows the edge of the display, so the buttons no longer get cut off on smaller watches.

Timers can keep alerting until you acknowledge them. Turn it on in Settings under Notifications and the end alert repeats until you tap Stop or Snooze. Your Apple Watch can alert first and your iPhone join in if you do not respond, and stopping on either device stops both. This is off by default.

Presentation mode is now called session mode. The feature is unchanged.

If you bought Pro before the price goes up you become a founding supporter. Every paid feature added from here on is yours at no extra cost.

When you finish a session we ask for the next date and remind you at 7pm the evening before.

Classes and workshops are now in the list of situations you pick from at first launch.

Fixed a bug where a timer started on the Watch restarted from the beginning after you quit and reopened the app. Also fixed Watch notifications appearing only in English, and a mistranslated message at the end of a timer.

Nothing changed about what we collect. Anonymous counts only. Timer names, alert messages and session scripts are never sent.
